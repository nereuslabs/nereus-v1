# 🌊 Nereus 2.0 Logo Concepts & Design Explorations

Here are the 4 curated pixel-art logo directions created for Nereus 2.0. Each represents a distinct philosophical dimension of an AI programming mentor.

---

## 🍾 Variant 1: "Ship in a Bottle" (The Sandboxed Explorer)
> **File**: [`assets/logo-ship-in-bottle.svg`](logo-ship-in-bottle.svg)  
> **Concept**: A miniature pixel-art brigantine sailing on neon cyan waves inside a self-contained glass bottle with a cork stopper and floating digital sparkle stars.

### Why it fits Nereus & Coding:
* **The Hermetic Sandbox**: In modern software engineering, sandboxing (Docker, WASM, hermetic testbeds) is fundamental. A ship in a bottle is the ultimate physical metaphor for a safe, self-contained micro-environment.
* **Intricate Craftsmanship**: Building a ship inside a bottle requires patience, precision, and care — the exact virtues of writing clean Rust code.
* **Poetic & Unique**: Almost no AI tools use a ship in a bottle; it immediately stands out in the GitHub ecosystem.

> **File**: [`assets/logo.svg`](logo.svg)  
> **Concept**: A glowing neon trident emerging from geometric ocean waves, crowned by a beacon star of truth.

* **Direct Mythology**: Nereus is the mythical Greek Titan of the Aegean Sea. The trident is the universal symbol of oceanic power and mastery.
* **Extreme Simplicity & Favicon Scaling**: Highly recognizable at tiny sizes (16x16 terminal icon or browser tab).

## 🧭 Variant 3: "The Compass Helm" (The Navigator's Guide)
> **File**: [`assets/logo-compass-helm.svg`](logo-compass-helm.svg)  
> **Concept**: A nautical captain's steering wheel integrated with a 4-point directional compass star and a glowing terminal cursor (`>`) in the center hub.

### Why it fits Nereus & Coding:
* **Setting the Course**: Nereus doesn't just evaluate answers; it builds your **Roadmap** and steers your learning path.
* **The Terminal Prompt**: The center hub contains a digital terminal prompt (`>`), bridging maritime navigation with command-line mastery.

## 🗼 Variant 4: "The Abyssal Beacon" (The Socratic Lighthouse)
> **File**: [`assets/logo-lighthouse.svg`](logo-lighthouse.svg)  
> **Concept**: A retro pixel-art lighthouse standing upon a rocky coastal foundation, casting an intense, focused beam of light into the dark sea.

### Why it fits Nereus & Coding:
* **Illuminating Dark Concepts**: Learning complex topics (pointers, borrow checker, concurrency) feels like navigating in thick fog. The lighthouse cuts through the obscurity.
* **Socratic Guidance**: A lighthouse doesn't push the boat; it shines light so the navigator can guide their own vessel.

### Terminal / Pixel Mockup:
```text

              [***]══════════► (Beam of Clarity)
              █████
              ░░░░░
              █████
             ░░░░░░░
            █████████
           ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓
       ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
```
